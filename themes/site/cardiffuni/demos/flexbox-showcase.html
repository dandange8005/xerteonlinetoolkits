Flexbox Utilities Showcase

Cardiff University Theme - Enhanced Flexbox System

💡 Recommended Approach

Use flex-basis utilities (`.flex-300`, `.flex-400`, etc.) for most layouts:

  • ✅ Simpler - No responsive classes or calc() needed
  • ✅ Gap-friendly - Works perfectly with any gap size
  • ✅ Auto-responsive - Automatically wraps when needed
  • ✅ Flexible - Items grow to fill available space

Use percentage utilities (`.w-md-50`, `.w-md-33`) only when you need exact percentage splits with gap-md (16px).
