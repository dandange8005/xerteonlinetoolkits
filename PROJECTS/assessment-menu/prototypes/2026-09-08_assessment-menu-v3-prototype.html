Assessment Menu

What each assessment type looks like in Lane 1 and in Lane 2 — and what you would change to move it.

Prototype v3 · 8 September 2026 · not a published resource
Guidance, not policy. This menu does not say which assessment type you should use, or which lane your assessment belongs in. Those decisions sit with programme teams, judged against your learning outcomes. Use the operational guide's four questions to decide the lane, then use this menu to see what that decision means in practice.

Lane 1 — verified

Establishes that the student has achieved the outcome. Carries at least one assurance feature: supervision, observation, or an accountability moment where they explain their work.

Lane 2 — open

Learning with AI available, scaffolded and made visible. Not lower-integrity assessment — judgement, source use and transparency are what get marked.
