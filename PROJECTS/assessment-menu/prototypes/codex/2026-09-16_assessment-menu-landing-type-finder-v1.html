    Think across the programme

Two lanes. Complementary purposes.

Lane 1

Secure assurance of learning

Trustworthy evidence of what students have achieved. Focus on verification of learning outcomes; this does not have to mean an exam.

Lane 2

Open assessment for and as learning

Assessment in open settings that supports learning, including responsible use of AI where the brief allows it.

What you are assessing determines the lane, not the assessment type. Both lanes are robust, and there is no set quota for Lane 1.

Cardiff’s approach ↗

AI, inclusion and student choice

Proposed wording · for discussion

The two-lane approach does not assume students must use generative AI. The proposed position is that learning outcomes should remain achievable without it and students should not be disadvantaged if they choose not to use it.

Make the role of AI clear in the assessment brief.

AI and assessment design ↗

Think across the programme

Students experience assessment across a programme. Look for progression, a balance of assessment types, manageable deadlines and consistent expectations about AI.

Use the menu on your own, or as a starting point for a discussion with your module or programme team.
