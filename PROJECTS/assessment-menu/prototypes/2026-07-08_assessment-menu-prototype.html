Assessment Menu

A design tool to support thoughtful choices about assessment purpose, authenticity, inclusivity, and the role of AI across a programme — built around the Two-Lane approach.

PROTOTYPE — for discussion, not a live resource
This menu is guidance, not policy. It is not a list of recommended replacements and it does not mandate any assessment type. Lane decisions sit with programme teams, made against learning outcomes. Use it to support redesign conversations.

Lane 1 — Verified

Secure assurance of learning. Lane 1 assessments verify that students have achieved the intended capabilities. Every Lane 1 component has at least one assurance feature: controlled conditions, contextualised task design, or a verification mechanism (e.g. viva, Q&A, live demonstration).

The aim is verification of learning outcomes — not making artefacts "AI-proof".

Lane 2 — Open

Open, scaffolded, feedback-oriented. Lane 2 assessments support learning with AI in the open: use is expected, made visible, and often assessed. Students develop ethical, critical and reflective AI practices.

Lane 2 is not lower-integrity assessment — it assesses different things, differently.

How to use this menu

Search or browse every assessment type in one flat list below — no need to guess which category a type sits under, and some types appear under more than one category. Each entry covers what the assessment is, what it's for, how to design it for each lane, practical considerations, authenticity, and inclusive design.

  • Categories are signposts, not silos — filter by category or ignore them entirely.
  • Each assessment type includes worked Lane 1 and Lane 2 designs side by side.
  • The design test: from any brief, a student should immediately know which lane, what that means for AI use, and how their judgement will be assessed.

How to decide the lane

The lane attaches to what is being assessed, not to the assessment format. Ask three questions:

1

Purpose

What learning outcome is this task assessing? Would AI use stop you making a trustworthy judgement about it?

2

Evidence

What evidence do you need that the capability is the student's own? Where does that evidence come from?

3

Environment

What conditions does the task need — and is at least one assurance feature present if it must be Lane 1?
