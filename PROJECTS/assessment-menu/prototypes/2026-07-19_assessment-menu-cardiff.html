Assessment Menu

At Cardiff University, effective assessment is guided by principles that help ensure tasks are meaningful, inclusive, and aligned with what we want students to know and be able to do. As you explore this menu, consider how your assessment design supports authenticity, sustainability, the balance of assessment for/as/of learning, responsible use of AI, and academic integrity.

This menu is not a list of recommended replacements. It is a design tool to support thoughtful choices about assessment purpose, authenticity, inclusivity, and the role of AI across a programme.

Use this menu to

  1. Browse assessment families.
  2. Search by purpose, AI use, authenticity, or accessibility.
  3. Compare up to three assessment types.
