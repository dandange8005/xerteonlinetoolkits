Our review of research (Click here to access) found that people respond differently to different types of resources.

Online

People enjoy using resources on the internet, if they were recommended by a healthcare professional so they could be trusted.

Hard Copies

Others would rather read leaflets given to them before prehabilitation, or as part of a prehabilitation package.

Healthcare professionals in our case study research said they were aware resources needed to be tailored to the individual:​
