Supervised in-person prehab is considered best, although time, transport and health can be barriers to access (Click here to access relevant research article).​

Some prehab services offer ambulance transport or offer prehab appointments alongside other appointments. ​

Making sure prehab includes activities that meet patients’ preferences is important.

Home-based prehabilitation

Exercising privately and safely at home could help with overcoming self-consciousness, giving a sense of control and integrating interventions into everyday lives.

Remote support

Home-based prehab supported remotely were convenient and reduced transport-related costs and barriers.

Barriers to home-based prehabilitation

Linking to past personal activities or behaviours can make prehab more achievable.

Physical discomfort with exercise equipment, dislike of food supplements, limited participation.
