Physical & emotional capacity​

Click the arrows to move between cards, or the dots to jump to a section.

Burden of cancer and co-morbidities

“It took me ages to get through to them that it’s not my breathing that stops me functioning, it’s the pain in the back.”
“I've got paperwork coming out of my ears here from different things between the financial side of the Universal Credit and all the other appointments.”

Physical ability

“We’ve had people who physically just can’t manage.”

Anxiety and/or low mood

Patient says they are doing everything to be fit and every time doctors tell them that they have to be stronger, they get anxious. Patient says, "I am really trying but all this is too much." ​

Work

“I worked and worked, right up to the, the Saturday before the operation.”

Carer role

“She’s a full-time carer for her daughter so that obviously takes up quite a lot of her day.”​
“I don't want to take on too much. But yeah, because I know after this, it's going to hit the rest of the family isn’t it. So, I'm trying to keep everything as normal as possible.”

Accepting cancer diagnosis

“She was really struggling with the diagnosis because she couldn't understand why it was her and …why she didn't have any side effects or issues. So, it was very much of a shock to her.”

Physical and social environment

Click the arrows to move between cards, or the dots to jump to a section.

Stigma

"He said he felt embarrassed that he had cancer."

Transport

“I would rather spend those five hours … going out doing walking and exercising at home than spending a lot of it, a lot of the time sitting on a bus or hanging around waiting for buses that tend, most of the time, not, not to turn up.”
“He can’t drive himself, he’s reliant on his brother who works shifts.”

Isolation

Patient explains that “it gets real (at prehab) because you can talk about it (cancer) here." Patient doesn’t talk about cancer at home… ​
“I think he’s quite a lonely gentleman and he does suffer a little bit with his mental health.” ​

Access to equipment

“My husband looks after our baby while I go to the gym.”

Social support

“I’ve got a husband supporting me through this, but with a little kid, we need a bigger support network…”​ ​
