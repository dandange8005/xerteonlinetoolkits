About Bootstrap Components

The Bootstrap 2 components Xerte's player loads, as cardiffuniversity restyles them (scss/_bootstrap-components.scss). The Cardiff components (buttons, cards, callouts, rules and others) are on the Components page; tables are on Lists & Tables.

Alerts

Alert Variants

A grey surface, square edges and a 4px rule in the status colour, like the design system's callouts. The glyph and the bold word carry the status, so the rule's colour is never the only cue. Danger and error use the design-system error red.

Success! Your changes have been saved successfully.
Info: This course will be available from September 2025.
Warning! Your session will expire in 5 minutes.
Error! Unable to submit form. Please check required fields.
.alert .alert-success | .alert-info | .alert-warning | .alert-danger
Block Alert with Dismissible

Important Information

This is a block-level alert with more content. It can contain multiple paragraphs and additional formatting.

Click the × button to dismiss this alert.

.alert .alert-block + .close[data-dismiss="alert"]

Panels

Panel with a title label
The label comes from the title attribute. The frame is square and grey-30; the label is grey-70 on the surface grey (5.13:1).

Panel content. The player draws the frame and echoes the title into the corner label; the theme sets their colours, corners and padding.

Usage Guidelines

Alerts
  • Success: Completed actions
  • Info: Neutral information
  • Warning: Caution needed
  • Danger: Errors, critical issues
Badges vs Labels
  • Badges: Counts, notifications
  • Labels: Categories, status tags
  • Use sparingly to avoid clutter
Accessibility
  • Alerts use semantic color + icons
  • Accordions use ARIA expanded states
